CISD Performance Review

An independent resource tracking Conroe ISD's academic performance in plain language. Explore TEA accountability grades, STAAR results, graduation, and CCMR for the district and all 66 campuses — plus a year-by-year audit of what CISD committed to in its District Improvement Plan versus what state data actually shows.

Definitions & Acronyms Common TEA terms and acronyms
▼
TEA
Texas Education Agency — the state agency that oversees public K–12 education in Texas, publishes TAPR data, and assigns A–F accountability ratings.
TAPR
Texas Academic Performance Report — TEA's annual district- and campus-level data release covering STAAR results, graduation, CCMR, attendance, and more.
DIP
District Improvement Plan — CISD's annually adopted plan with measurable performance objectives, strategies, and formative review checkpoints for each school year.
STAAR
State of Texas Assessments of Academic Readiness — standardized exams administered in grades 3–8 and via End-of-Course (EOC) tests in high school.
EOC
End of Course — high school STAAR exams required for graduation: English I, English II, Algebra I, Biology, and U.S. History.
Approaches GL
Approaches Grade Level — the minimum passing standard on STAAR; students scoring at this level are considered on track at a basic level.
Meets GL
Meets Grade Level — the intermediate STAAR standard indicating solid grade-level performance; used most often in DIP targets.
Masters GL
Masters Grade Level — the highest STAAR performance level, indicating advanced understanding of grade-level content.
CCMR
College, Career, & Military Readiness — the percentage of graduates who meet at least one readiness indicator (TSI scores, AP/IB exams, industry certifications, or military enlistment).
Econ. Disadvantaged
Students who qualify for free or reduced-price meals, or who have been identified as economically disadvantaged through other state criteria.
Emergent Bilingual / ELL
Students identified as English Language Learners (ELL) who are currently receiving bilingual or ESL services. Also labeled "EB/EL (Current)" in TAPR data.
Special Education
Students who receive services under an Individualized Education Program (IEP). Also labeled "Special Ed (Current)" in TAPR data.
TxCHSE
Texas Certificate of High School Equivalency — a GED-equivalent credential counted alongside traditional graduates in Texas graduation rate calculations.
A–F Rating
TEA's annual district and campus accountability rating (A through F) based on three domains: Student Achievement, School Progress, and Closing the Gaps.
Formative Review
Mid-year progress checkpoints (December, March, June) within the DIP where each strategy is rated: No Progress, Some Progress, Considerable, Accomplished, Continue/Modify, or Discontinue.
Divergence
A flag on this site indicating that CISD self-reported progress or success on an objective, but TEA state data shows the numeric target was not met.
